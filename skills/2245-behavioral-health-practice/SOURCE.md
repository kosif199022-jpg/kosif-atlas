# behavioral-health-practice

Behavioral Health Practice specialist team — agents (behavioral-health-practice-lead, intake-access-analyst, clinical-documentation-compliance-specialist, payer-billing-specialist), skills, templates, commands, an advisory hook, best-practice rules, and a research-grounded knowledge bank. An operations team for a practice administrator or clinical-ops lead accountable for access, utilization, documentation compliance, and margin — it manages no-shows as a flow, reads intake-to-first-appointment access time as the conversion lever, ties documentation to compliance and billing, staffs caseload to demand, and reads payer mix as the margin driver. Inherits ravenclaude-core protocols.
