# leadfeeder

Turn website visitor signals into pipeline. Six Leadfeeder-powered skills — Getting Started, Daily Visitor Brief, Visitor Company Brief, Outreach Companion, Find My Buyer, and Add to Pipeline — let you identify high-intent accounts, research buyers, find the right contacts, personalise outreach, and add companies to your lists, all from within Claude. New here? Type "Set up my Leadfeeder brief" to run a 30-second readiness check first.
