# carrel-mail

Email archives with the carrel CLI: /mail saves attachments, splits an mbox into messages, groups eml/mbox files into threads and converts Outlook .pst exports; eml/mbox are first-class desk files for index, search, pack, refs and the Read guard; plus a mail-archive skill.
