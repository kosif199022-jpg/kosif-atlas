# gemini-core

Keeps the Gemini keys and tier of every Gemini mod, and each mod's model and thinking level, in one place: adds $.gemini, which builds their Gemini requests, reads the answers and moves to the next key after a quota or key error, and picks a model from Google's list. Needs function hooks (early access).
