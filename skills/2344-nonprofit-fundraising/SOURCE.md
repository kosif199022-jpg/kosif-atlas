# nonprofit-fundraising

Nonprofit Fundraising specialist team — agents (development-lead, grant-writer, major-gifts-strategist, nonprofit-finance-analyst), skills, templates, commands, an advisory hook, best-practice rules, and a research-grounded knowledge bank covering both pre-award development and post-award grant compliance. A development team for a nonprofit fundraiser or executive director — it protects donor retention (the cheapest dollar a nonprofit has, at ~$0.20 to keep vs ~$1.50 to acquire), builds the grant pipeline on fit before effort, segments the donor base by value and recency, and reads cost-to-raise-a-dollar honestly across channels. Inherits ravenclaude-core protocols.
