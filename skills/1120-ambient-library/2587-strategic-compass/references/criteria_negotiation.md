# Negotiation Decision Scoring Criteria

Load this when the decision involves another party — a negotiation, partnership
discussion, conflict, or situation where the outcome depends on another actor's response.

Additional scoring dimensions:

| Criterion | What to evaluate |
|---|---|
| BATNA strength | How good is the user's outside option if this fails? |
| Leverage asymmetry | Who needs this more? |
| Relationship capital | What does each path cost in relationship terms? |
| Information advantage | What does the other party not know that matters? |
| Timeline pressure | Who is more time-sensitive? |

In the Branch Expansion step, add:
- "Their most likely counter-move" for each branch
- "What they know vs. what they don't know"
- "Walk-away trigger: what condition causes the user to exit"
