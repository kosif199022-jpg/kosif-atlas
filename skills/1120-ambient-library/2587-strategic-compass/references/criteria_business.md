# Default Business Decision Scoring Criteria

Use these when the user has not stated their own criteria.

| Criterion | Weight | What to evaluate |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to outcome | High | How fast does cash flow or results appear? |
| Capital efficiency | High | Cost to test vs. cost to scale |
| Risk profile | Medium | Downside if wrong; reversibility |
| Strategic optionality | Medium | Does this foreclose other moves? |
| Founder/leader fit | Medium | Does this play to their stated strengths? |

Adjust weights based on context:
- Early-stage company: weight capital efficiency and speed highest
- Established business: weight strategic optionality and risk higher
- Distressed situation: weight speed and reversibility highest
