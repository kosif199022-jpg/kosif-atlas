# dental-practice

Dental Practice specialist team — agents (dental-practice-lead, clinical-treatment-planner, dental-rcm-specialist, dental-operations-analyst), skills, templates, commands, an advisory hook, best-practice rules, and a research-grounded knowledge bank. A treatment-planning-and-revenue-cycle team for a dental practice owner — it controls overhead against the ~62% median, holds collections above 98%, builds case acceptance on the treatment plan rather than the discount, and reads doctor/hygiene production per hour the way a practice that runs on the schedule does. Inherits ravenclaude-core protocols.
