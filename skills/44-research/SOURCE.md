# research

Deep web research (any topic) modelled on the commercial deep-research products: /research:team-research clarifies scope only when needed, shows a plan of sub-questions for approval, spawns one iterative deep-researcher per sub-question in parallel, verifies contradictions with quick-searcher, synthesizes a long-form cited report and writes it to disk; tiers quick/standard/deep scale effort; optional serper.dev backend via websearch.py when SERPER_API_KEY is set, native WebSearch otherwise. quick-searcher (sonnet) also answers single-fact lookups directly
