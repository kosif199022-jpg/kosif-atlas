# carrel-finance

Accounting desk work with the carrel CLI: /refs finds invoice, PO, IBAN, routing, VAT and tracking numbers across files, tags files with them, and shows which documents share a reference; /fields extracts vendor, dates, totals and currency from invoices, receipts and statements. Plus a bookkeeper agent and an accounting-inbox skill.
