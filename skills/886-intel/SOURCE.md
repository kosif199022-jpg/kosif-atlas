# intel

Gather and distill knowledge from long-form sources and from what people say: transcribe audio locally with whisper, turn an article, podcast transcript, or video into durable, searchable highlights, translate a whole English EPUB book into a Chinese PDF in the original's format, find EPUB download links and download the book, analyze an app's App Store reviews into a data-driven Chinese doc with charts, brainstorm a brand name whose domain is actually registrable, and research one creator into a sourced, adversarially reviewed case study PDF. Fetch and analyze X mentions, posts and user timelines, profile users, collect TikTok mentions and fetch App Store reviews.
