# governed-second-brain

Bob's Big Brain — the governed team brain: cited recall, hash-chained receipts. ONE plugin, two modes. Local (default): turn your own files into cited (qmd://) memory with deterministic governance and a tamper-evident SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail, in-process with no runtime network after one-time native dependency provisioning. Team (set TEAMKB_API_URL): query your team's single governed brain over the tailnet with a per-user token.
