# film-video-production

Film & Video Production specialist team — agents (production-lead, line-producer, post-production-supervisor, production-finance-analyst), skills, templates, commands, an advisory hook, best-practice rules, a research-grounded knowledge bank with Mermaid decision trees, a scenarios bank, and a stdlib production-economics calculator (shoot-day cost, contingency burn, overtime burden). A production-management team for a producer, production company, or post house — it budgets to a defensible top-sheet, schedules to the shoot day rather than the calendar, runs the post pipeline as a dependency chain, and reads production economics the way a line producer who answers for every dollar on the day must. Inherits ravenclaude-core protocols.
