# p-hacking-skills

Study p-hacking in econometric designs: map researcher degrees of freedom, audit a specification search with a complete ledger and null-calibrated honest p-values, replay realistic search procedures, detect p-hacking in a literature, immunise an analysis, and benchmark whether an AI agent p-hacks. Research and teaching use only.
