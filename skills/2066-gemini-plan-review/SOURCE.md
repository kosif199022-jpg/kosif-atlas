# gemini-plan-review

Has Gemini review each plan before the approval dialog, from the plan and the conversation, and sends a plan with a blocking finding back to the model, at most twice. Off until /gemini-plan-review on; gemini-core holds the key, tier, model and thinking level. Needs function hooks (early access).
