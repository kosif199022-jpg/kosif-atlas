# customer-support-cx-operations

Customer Support & CX Operations specialist team — agents (support-ops-lead, ticket-deflection-analyst, queue-staffing-specialist, csat-quality-strategist), skills, templates, commands, an advisory hook, best-practice rules, and a research-grounded knowledge bank. A support-ops team for a head of support, CX manager, or founder accountable for cost-to-serve and SLA attainment — it deflects before hiring, staffs to forecast and occupancy, reads CSAT segmented, and treats backlog as arrivals-against-capacity flow. Inherits ravenclaude-core protocols.
