# comment-slop

Find and remove redundant comments and docstring content while retaining rationale, contracts, and documentation/tooling functions. Apply a source-based keep/delete/reduce/rewrite review; optionally consult Jev on redundancy and whether a proposed edit loses useful information. Reuses the configurable general Jev helper and skips it without helper or credentials. Use when cleaning up AI-slop comments, auditing a diff, reducing mixed docstrings, or checking a comment rewrite.
