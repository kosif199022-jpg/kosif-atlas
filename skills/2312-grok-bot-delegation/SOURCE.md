# grok-bot-delegation

Grok Bot delegation SOP for Chief of Staff — on every Matthew ask: match or create purpose-built expert bots (via grok-bot-creation), port or upstream RavenClaude skills, SendToAgent with a tight brief, then coordinate; escalate to Matthew only for decisions, auth, or irreversible actions. Also carries the fleet token-spend playbook: condensed returns, an effort ladder before spawn, routine hygiene, connectors over browser, CPCT measurement. Distinct from ravenclaude-core Team Lead orchestration (Claude Code) and ai-agent-engineering (building agentic products). Requires ravenclaude-core@>=0.7.0.
