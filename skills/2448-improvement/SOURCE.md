# improvement

Evidence-first, cross-dimension improvement finder. Point it at a repo, feature, concept, or process surface and it produces a ranked, evidence-cited list of improvement candidates led by value-to-effort, interviews on the pick, and hands off to the planning pipeline; runnable unattended as a tech-debt-sweep routine.
