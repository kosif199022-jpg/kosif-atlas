# retail-store-operations

Brick-and-mortar retail store operations team — agents (store-ops-lead, merchandising-analyst, inventory-and-replenishment-analyst, labor-scheduling-analyst, loss-prevention-advisor) covering four-wall P&L and store KPIs, assortment and planogram compliance, inventory accuracy and replenishment, labor scheduling to traffic, and shrink root-cause and loss prevention. skills, commands, templates, Mermaid decision-tree knowledge bank + 2026 capability map, best-practices, and an advisory hook. Seams: online/DTC -> ecommerce-dtc, upstream demand planning -> supply-chain-planning, buying -> procurement-sourcing. Requires ravenclaude-core@>=0.7.0.
