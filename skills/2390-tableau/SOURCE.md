Citation-grounded knowledge bank with Mermaid decision trees + a best-practices library. Seams: Salesforce platform data → salesforce; warehouse/semantic modeling → data-platform/microsoft-fabric; Power BI comparison → power-platform; methodology/security-review → ravenclaude-core. Requires ravenclaude-core@>=0.7.0.
