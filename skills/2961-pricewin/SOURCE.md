# pricewin

Live hotel and flight prices compared across Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com and Traveloka, in USD, through the PriceWin MCP server. Includes the pricewin-travel-search skill. Can send a booking request to a direct-listed hotel (nothing is charged; the guest pays at the property).
