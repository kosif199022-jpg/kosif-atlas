# verification

Outcome-verification stage: prove a change achieved its intended outcome (`/verification:confirm`: a mechanical build/test/lint prerequisite gate, then intent-match + evidence + verdict with the criterion auto-detected by change type), and verify measurable-improvement claims against a planning-time baseline (`/verification:measure`), never fabricating numbers.
