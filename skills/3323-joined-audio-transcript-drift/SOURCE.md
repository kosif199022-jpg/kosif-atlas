# joined-audio-transcript-drift

Keep timestamps aligned when joining many audio files into one: a lossless concat keeps every piece's gapless padding, so a transcript built from measured durations drifts later and later. Covers why frame counts do not fix it, when to encode once, and how to verify drift.
