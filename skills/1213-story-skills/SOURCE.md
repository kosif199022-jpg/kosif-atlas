# story-skills

A suite of cross-referenced skills and CLI maintenance tools for end-to-end story writing powered by markdown. Covers premise development, story initialization, character management, worldbuilding, plot structure, chapter writing, verse, line editing, revision passes, continuity, pacing and fair-play checks, editorial review, research, publishing and print production, adaptation, validation, reporting, indexing, word counts, and manuscript export.
