## What is and is not included

- Runnable original HTML/CSS/JS structure examples, desktop geometry, original mobile adaptations, and a small working parser with an invalid-input state.
- No proprietary fonts, images, copied marketing text, source bundles, or reference screenshots in the public library.
- Ditto jobs were explicitly resubmitted on September 30. At initial library creation all were still queued. The former Cursor job returned 404. No app-source inspection is claimed until code is actually received and read.
- Ditto output, when available, is reconstruction code—not proof of a site's original framework or internals. Extract hierarchy, nested UI structure, component responsibilities and breakpoint logic; check fidelity against the live page. Do not publish raw branded output as the reusable library.

## Why the earlier examples failed

Cadence and the first TRACE iteration repeated the same skeleton: two-line oversized proposition, bordered demo, dominant card plus two small cards, epigrammatic section headings, closing slogan. Two fonts and edge fades did not change that composition. Those iterations were rejected by the user; do not cite their build success as design validation.

For a reference-led task, compare actual relationships before decorating: headline-to-product area, UI text size, position of copy relative to media, density inside and outside the product, and how one section transitions into the next. Then choose the relevant structure, not all three. Do not turn Cursor's 26px headline into a universal rule.
