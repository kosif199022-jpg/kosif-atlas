# Audit and evaluation record

## Initial audit — 2026-09-29

An independent Astra audit found six material gaps: unsupported claims of exhaustive reference research; abstract guidance without composition detail; one generic aesthetic repeated across categories; an ornamental Northstar dashboard; review questions with no evidence requirement; and accessibility/integration gaps in borrowable code.

The initial published research claims were too strong. Seed HTML, sitemap discovery, and queued Ditto jobs did not demonstrate that every main-navigation page had been studied. The revised ledger reports retrieval and visual inspection separately. Incomplete discovery and blocked destinations remain visible.

## Matched evaluation protocol — not yet executed

To evaluate against Claude's default frontend skill, use the same model, model settings, time allowance, tool access, asset budget, and brief. Vary only the skill. Keep trial output isolated from this library so the second condition cannot see the first output. Use at least one SaaS, commerce, developer, and research brief; repeat each because a single generation is noisy.

Suggested briefs:

1. A workspace for customer-support teams to turn repeat incidents into product decisions; must show a disagreement and a review trigger.
2. A repairable desk fan with three finishes; must show scale, technical details, stock, delivery, and returns.
3. A webhook delivery service; must demonstrate a request, successful delivery, retry, and rejection.
4. A public-interest research group studying urban heat; must connect a thesis, annotated evidence, limitations, methods, and publications.

Before seeing outputs, establish a rubric: product understanding (25%), hierarchy and composition (25%), distinctive and coherent identity (20%), interaction usefulness (15%), responsive/accessibility quality (15%). Review anonymized desktop/mobile captures and recorded interaction states in randomized order. Use independent reviewers, including the user, and collect reasons as well as scores. Record disqualifying failures separately from taste ratings.

Publish the prompts, environment, outputs, rubric, reviewer count, per-brief scores, and limitations. Do not claim superiority based on this skill's own revised example or on an author's self-rating. The current repository makes no validated superiority claim.
