# Visual studies — 29 September 2026

These observations come from inspected desktop captures and rendered DOM measurements, mostly at **1440 × 1000**. They describe this snapshot, not permanent brand rules. Heading boxes are DOM bounds, not necessarily the ink width. Screenshots captured before late assets settle can be incomplete. Mobile behavior and inner-page interactions require separate evidence; do not infer them from desktop measurements.

## ElevenLabs

[Homepage](https://elevenlabs.io/): the main content begins at x=132 with two 564px columns. The 48px headline has roughly three times the scale of the 16px platform explanation. A full-width segmented product selector follows the text; media is the largest object in the first viewport. Three expanded menus exposed 73 distinct navigation links across product, solution, and resource states.

**Transfer:** for a broad creative platform, group related capabilities into a few worlds before exposing the full catalog. Make the representative output actionable. Build a segmented state model with consistent shell dimensions, then change the content and medium inside it. A colored sphere without an output would omit the useful part. Audio playback was not tested in this study.

## Linear

[Homepage](https://linear.app/): 64px primary type, approximately 48px chapter headings, and near-black `rgb(8,9,10)` canvas. At 1440px, chapter text begins around x=78 with a roughly 542px title column. The product occupies a broad horizontal stage rather than a small floating dashboard. Intake, planning, AI, and shipping each show their own concrete work objects.

**Transfer:** repeat actual artifacts through a workflow. Distinct task states create section variety while a persistent grid creates identity. Product and Resources menus reveal grouped destinations and secondary links; don't mistake the collapsed header for the complete navigation. Northstar borrows the continuity of evidence, not the dark interface skin. Its dissent note plays a role comparable to a real state change in a workflow.

## Stripe

[Spain/English homepage](https://stripe.com/en-es): visible vertical rules establish a grid around a long 48px proposition. Its text box starts around x=208 and spans about 959px. The expressive color field enters from the upper right and extends beyond the hero, while the text and actions keep clear alignment. Subsequent 32px section headings are quieter. Four expanded menus exposed 87 destinations.

**Transfer:** combine stable commercial architecture with one expressive layer. Keep product taxonomy, customer evidence, developer detail, and buying actions in different sections. Do not convert every chapter into equally weighted cards. The locale matters: record it with the URL and avoid mixing pricing or terms from another region. Financial claims were not independently verified here.

## Supabase

[Homepage](https://supabase.com/): measured 46px two-line promise, 16px product titles, 34px closing/open-source headings. The 1440px layout starts at x=176; a 536px headline column sits beside explanatory copy. The database tile spans more grid area than the adjacent primitives, reflecting its foundational role. The captured theme is light, not the dark theme commonly associated with developer tools.

**Transfer:** give the foundational primitive more visual weight; organize the remaining tools by job. Product detail belongs inside the tile, not merely an icon and adjective. Three expanded menus exposed 51 unique destinations. Do not hardcode “developer site = dark mode.” Theme, density, and hierarchy are separate choices.

## Cloudflare

[Homepage](https://www.cloudflare.com/): an almost full-width orange field frames a centered 56px claim within a 1080px text box. A narrow page margin and inset corner treatment make the hero feel like one large surface. Lower chapters shift to network scale, customer adoption, and usage pricing.

**Transfer:** a platform spanning many categories needs an organizing thesis before its catalog. Use a large systems visual when it explains the infrastructure. Header discovery was incomplete in this capture: only 10 navigation destinations were found. The captured first viewport omitted the full product menu, so this study cannot certify navigation completeness. Cookies and late-loaded UI were visible capture limitations.

## Firecrawl

## Cursor

[Homepage](https://cursor.com/): its main proposition is only 26px, starting around x=70. The large product stage begins below two restrained actions and uses much more area than the headline. The background is warm `rgb(247,247,244)`. Inner product UI and task content, not enormous display text, supply the density.

**Transfer:** use small typography confidently when the product demonstration carries the argument. An oversized headline is not a prerequisite for premium design. The screenshot overlays desktop and CLI contexts without losing the task. Preserve readable UI and choose a focal job; a generic empty editor is not equivalent proof. Some pricing/enterprise destinations were rate-limited during capture.

## Mercury

[Homepage](https://mercury.com/): centered approximately 49px title and 672px text column over a landscape scene. A desk and laptop provide a scale anchor within the environment. The signup field and demo action sit above the horizon; a compact legal disclosure remains in the hero region. Lower sections introduce concrete banking jobs.

**Transfer:** art-directed imagery can communicate the customer's desired life, but it must remain connected to product operations and trust information. Plan the horizon, text-safe area, focal object, and disclosure placement as one composition. A generic stock landscape alone does not create this relationship. This is a design observation, not verification of the financial claims.

## Prism Science

[Homepage](https://prismscience.org/): roughly 89px primary type at 1440px; chapter headings around 57px. A pale field and molecular object connect the subject to the visual system. Later large text shifts across the horizontal grid rather than occupying the same centered column repeatedly. The narrative moves from invisible motion to measurement, modeling, infrastructure, and impact.

**Transfer:** let the scientific question govern the visual metaphor and section sequence. Use motion only where it clarifies change. Fieldwork translates this principle into an inspectable synthetic signal, not a copied molecule. The source's long scroll narrative was not fully motion-tested; static captures do not prove its choreography.

## Content Architecture

[Homepage](https://www.contentarchitecture.dev/): an approximately 43px title in a 511px left column faces a full-height typographic field on the right. Mono navigation, file-like labels, stack facts, and technical vocabulary make the repository metaphor coherent. The page alternates the sides and density of explanatory chapters. In-page navigation anchors are preserved in the ledger.

**Transfer:** a governing metaphor should influence navigation, content, proof, and behavior together. Build the technical artifact itself—schema, file tree, editable output—before a decorative terminal frame. The captured animation is a visual state; implementation and motion timing were not recovered from a screenshot.

## Blink

[Homepage](https://www.blink.trade/): a sparse network diagram dominates the left and upper field; a roughly 60px proposition sits in a lower-right panel starting near x=776. Monospaced labels name physical relationships, and the page later expands the mechanism through short chapters. The result is deliberately unlike a conventional centered launch hero.

**Transfer:** place text around the thing being explained. When the proposition is spatial, use labeled distance, routing, or topology instead of a decorative glow. Keep a simple reading path and an obvious action. This capture does not verify exchange claims, regulatory status, or real-world network performance.

## Edoardo Lunardi / Prism case study

[Case study](https://www.edoardolunardi.dev/works/prism): a dense dark workbench with a project index on the left, metadata in the middle, and a two-column artifact area on the right. The project heading is only about 14px. The shell places comparison and browsing ahead of a large hero. Some images were still visibly soft in the captured state.

**Transfer:** experimental portfolios can prioritize rapid inspection through persistent navigation and explicit role/stack/year metadata. Desktop density should not simply shrink onto mobile; mobile recomposition remains to be studied here.

## What remains unproven

The ledger records hundreds of discovered destinations and their retrieval/capture states. That breadth is not equivalent to detailed visual, mobile, and interaction studies for every page. Current measurements support these specific observations. Full navigation closure, all inner-page state reviews, and comparative superiority to a default skill remain open work.
