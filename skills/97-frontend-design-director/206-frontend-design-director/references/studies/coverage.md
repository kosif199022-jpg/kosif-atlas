# Navigation coverage ledger

Snapshot: 29 September 2026. **The accessible breadth pass is substantially expanded; comprehensive research remains incomplete.** 550 discovered URLs include anchors, external destinations, account entries, and duplicate page bodies—not 550 unique templates or completed studies.

All 333 previously unreviewed captures were inspected as opening contact sheets. A further 156 destinations were attempted: 148 produced full-height captures, six produced opening captures on retry, and two redirected to authentication origins blocked by auto-review. All successful captures were inspected, but full-height capture is not full-page review: this pass sampled opening, middle, and end. Some captures contain unloaded media, and some Cloudflare captures used a narrow viewport.

## Known gaps

- Perplexity access challenges, Ramp machine-text responses, HTTP failures, and loading shells are not counted as settled visual evidence.
- Pear source analysis and limited live observations exist; complete chapter/mobile inspection remains incomplete after a browser permission dismissal.
- Cursor Agents and Cloudflare Support redirected to private authentication origins. Auto-review denied access; no workaround or sign-in was attempted.
- Cloudflare Products/Solutions/Resources added 73 previously missed URLs. Retool Audience links matched the existing inventory. Other site navigation inventories are not independently certified exhaustive.
- Contact-sheet sampling does not verify every section, microinteraction, animation, responsive state, or accessibility requirement. Blank media does not imply an intentionally empty composition.
- External repository/docs/social destinations are recorded separately from marketing-page inspiration.
