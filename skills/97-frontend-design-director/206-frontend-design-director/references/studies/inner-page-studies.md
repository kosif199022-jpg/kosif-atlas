# Inner-page composition studies

Observed 29 September 2026, desktop at 1440 × 1000. These are first-viewport visual reviews with rendered heading sequences, not complete interaction audits. Cookie overlays, unloaded media, and blocked captures constrain the observations. Measurements are snapshot-specific. Prices and claims are not verified purchasing guidance.

## Pricing is a decision interface, not one universal card pattern

- [Linear pricing](https://linear.app/pricing): a restrained 48px heading begins at x=48. Four 24px plan headings sit in equal columns separated by hairlines, without floating card containers. Baseline-aligned actions make the different feature-list lengths comparable. The later feature matrix handles detail. **Borrow:** common comparison rows and stable action alignment. **Do not borrow blindly:** four columns on narrow screens or equal visual weight when one plan needs explanation.
- [Retool pricing](https://retool.com/pricing): a contiguous comparison table uses colored header bands to distinguish plans; the surrounding canvas stays quiet. Billing and hosting choices sit above the table. **Borrow:** encode meaningful categories in a shared structure, keeping the controls that change the comparison nearby. This review inspected the default state, not the billing calculations.
- [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/en-es/pricing): the opening choice is between standard and negotiated pricing, not an arbitrary three-tier ladder. A diagonal color ribbon passes behind two contrasting commercial surfaces; the grid and text remain stable. **Borrow:** start from the actual buying fork. The expressive layer should not obscure rates, conditions, or contact routes.
- [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing): four columns share clear unit/allowance lists. One raised, outlined plan marks the common entry point without applying heavy containers everywhere. **Borrow:** make units, limits, included usage, and overage legible before adding persuasion.
- [Firecrawl pricing](https://www.firecrawl.dev/pricing): a credit-volume control sits above tier options. The visitor's quantity is the organizing variable. **Borrow:** let a meaningful input drive the pricing story; when implementing, test input → estimate → tier consistency. The captured source slider was not exercised.
- [Mercury pricing](https://mercury.com/pricing): a centered introduction leads to a unified three-column comparison surface, with annual billing near the table and legal context visible at the bottom. **Borrow:** pair clarity of comparison with readable qualification; do not turn disclosures into decorative microtype.

## Product pages prove a narrower job

- [Cursor product](https://cursor.com/product): the 36px opening heading starts at x=70 with an 810px box. A large desktop/CLI artifact follows almost immediately. Subsequent 20–26px headings explain surfaces, codebase context, lifecycle, and engineering tools. **Borrow:** replace the marketing overview with a job-specific artifact and progressively explain its capabilities. Small headings can work when the artifact carries the visual weight.
- [Supabase database](https://supabase.com/database): persistent product subnavigation precedes a split heading/explanation row and a broad database table. The proof is the primitive itself, not an abstract illustration. **Borrow:** use representative fields and rows with enough density to explain the tool. Give the product family secondary navigation, distinct from the global menu.
- [Firecrawl scrape](https://www.firecrawl.dev/scrape): a tightly framed proposition leads into a code-output surface. The same instrument-grid language as the homepage is retained, but the content now explains one operation. **Borrow:** keep family identity while narrowing input, output, and documentation to the selected operation.
- [Stripe platforms](https://stripe.com/en-es/use-cases/platforms): a left-aligned category label, proposition, explanation, and two actions occupy one grid column; a slanted dark band anchors the opposite region. The capture did not show a complete product artifact there. **Borrow:** route by audience/job and preserve a strong text-safe column. Do not infer missing media or animation from this static capture.
- [Mercury business banking](https://mercury.com/business-banking): the general brand environment gives way to a centered proposition, account-opening controls, and a large accounts interface. **Borrow:** move from emotional positioning to operational proof on a product page. The legal layer stays part of the composition.
- [ElevenLabs enterprise](https://elevenlabs.io/enterprise): the opening centers the enterprise proposition and sales action, then introduces application surfaces below. **Borrow:** enterprise pages need a distinct evaluation path and enterprise-relevant evidence, not merely the homepage with a different CTA. This viewport alone does not establish all enterprise proof or security requirements.

## A shared identity can contain different information architectures

- [Prism science](https://prismscience.org/science): small left-rail classification, large offset thesis, and a dark chapter directly below. **Borrow:** let explanatory chapters change density and contrast while preserving an alignment system.
- [Prism research](https://prismscience.org/research): a large title faces explanatory copy, then filters and an editorial grid. **Borrow:** the research index needs discovery tools and metadata rather than another homepage narrative. Filters were observed, not tested.
- [Prism people](https://prismscience.org/people): an approximately 89px title sits above a light content sheet. Within it, a left group index faces a three-column portrait grid. Content starts around x=388 and spans 1004px. **Borrow:** make the institution's structure visible through grouping and navigation. On mobile, redesign the index relationship rather than shrinking the desktop rail.
- [Edoardo Lunardi works](https://www.edoardolunardi.dev/works): a compact persistent project list occupies the left third of a dark workbench; the remaining region is an inspection stage. In the captured default state that stage was largely empty. **Borrow:** rapid comparison and persistent context for a portfolio. Do not present the empty stage as an ideal first-visit state; loading, selection, and touch behavior still need evaluation.

## How to use these observations

Choose the decision or task first: compare plans, understand one operation, evaluate an institution, or inspect work. Then borrow the matching information structure. The same company uses different page grammars for different jobs. Palette similarity is weaker evidence of a good adaptation than a clear relationship between content, layout, and behavior.
