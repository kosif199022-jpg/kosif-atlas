## Application to the Cadence SaaS study

1. Deadline-led proposition with a wide, usable workspace: selected instead of an object-first 3D opening.
2. One sample project persists across timeline, task list, dependency explanation, and approval. A state change must propagate everywhere.
3. The middle chapter changes from dense interface to spacious mechanism explanation; the darker editorial chapter marks a pause, not another feature grid.
4. Review is explicit: resolve the outstanding task, then make a separate go/no-go decision. Approval does not publish anything.
5. Pricing is a transparent local calculation with illustrative units, not a fake purchase funnel.
6. **Maximum two font families**, following the user's latest preference. Variety comes from composition, scale, weight, and page purpose—not adding a third or fourth family.
7. DOM/SVG is sufficient for this workflow. Three.js remains available in the library for spatial stories such as LUMA; it is not a mandatory SaaS ornament.
