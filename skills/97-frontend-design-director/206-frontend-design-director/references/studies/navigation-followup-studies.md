# Navigation follow-up: structure, proof, and pacing

29 September 2026. Original analysis of the additional 156 navigation destinations attempted after the opening-capture backlog review. See [coverage](coverage.md) for per-URL evidence. Successful full-height captures were reviewed through opening/middle/end contact sheets, not every pixel of every page. Six retries supplied openings only. Unloaded media, authentication redirects, and skeletons are not treated as finished design. Motion described below as an adaptation is a proposal, not an observation of a reference animation.

These are optional composition ideas. Choose by the visitor's question and the product's proof burden; do not paste the same sequence into every project.

## Retool: a different proof format for each job

[Apps](https://retool.com/apps), [workflows](https://retool.com/workflows), and [AI](https://retool.com/ai) use a concrete product artifact near the opening proposition. Later chapters change the scale of evidence: workflow fragments, use cases, customer material, and commercial comparisons. Some lower media did not settle, so their final visual treatment remains unverified.

The mobile/external-app pages introduce physical operating contexts, then explain the software workflow. Self-hosting shifts toward deployment choices and commands. Integration and template catalogs instead use light backgrounds, persistent category controls, search, and repeated items: browsing is the primary task. CLI documentation uses navigation/article/code regions; it is not a marketing composition.

**Borrow:** let the same action progress from input → result → operating consequence. Separate catalogs, documentation, and persuasion pages rather than restyling one hero. For a sample approval workflow, display the blocker, the evidence required to clear it, and the separate final authorization. Cadence applies that distinction.

## ElevenLabs: let the output determine the stage

[Speech to text](https://elevenlabs.io/speech-to-text) puts a transcription mechanism before developer and enterprise paths. [Sound effects](https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects) pairs a prompt with a sample collection. [Music](https://elevenlabs.io/music) was opening-only on retry with partly unsettled media; it does not certify the final carousel behavior.

Image/video/avatars pages organize around output media, collections, and short making workflows. Mobile uses a phone-sized product stage and download context. Agent pages shift to a conversation/orb or chat artifact, then department use cases, operational controls, and trust. The integrations page is a category directory rather than another cinematic opening.

The startup-grant page introduces eligibility and a process; impact pages connect a mission to concrete organizations; safety pages organize principles and reporting tools. Blog, careers, webinar, and documentation destinations each use their own reading or browsing hierarchy. Blank or blurred v4/about/webinar media is an evidence limitation, not a whitespace recommendation.

**Borrow:** choose the proof object from what the user produces—transcript, sound, image, conversation, or operational result. Keep that object's identity through the story. Do not use a decorative orb as generic proof for unrelated software.

## Stripe: one commercial framework, several evidence scales

[Billing](https://stripe.com/billing) and subscription-related pages frame a system of commercial operations. Usage-billing proof centers on measurement and invoice relationships. [Financial Connections](https://stripe.com/financial-connections) explains connection and account-data capabilities through interface-sized objects. [Sigma](https://stripe.com/sigma) changes the proof format to a query workspace and questions the product can answer.

Payments, Terminal, Link, and Invoicing share a recognizable commercial grid, but their lower chapters differ: mechanism, physical implementation, code integration, or operational record. Opening product media was blank in some captures; no exact hero reconstruction follows from those captures.

Partner pages explain program types and benefits. The marketplace gives filters and listings priority. [Stripe.dev](https://stripe.dev/) is a distinct deep-blue editorial/developer index, combining features, categories, and technical media. Documentation captures sometimes contained nested scrolling; a short screenshot is not evidence of a short document.

**Borrow:** hold the grid steady while changing the evidence format to match the question. Commercial pages should make the buying/operating model inspectable; developer pages should expose actual inputs and outputs. Cadence's pricing calculator is illustrative, and its cost visibly depends on editor count and billing period.

## Cloudflare: primitives, architecture, and audience paths

The expanded Products/Solutions/Resources menus revealed 73 destinations absent from the earlier inventory. Many newer product pages share dark canvases, orange actions, compact category labels, and grouped benefits. The interesting variation is in the mechanism chapter, not another color palette.

- Compute pages explain relationships through isolate/container diagrams, workflow code, deployment context, and queues.
- Storage pages connect ingestion, catalog/query operations, use cases, and pricing units.
- AI pages use routing, agent relationships, code, and model-oriented controls. Several visual panels remained unloaded; animation and rendering technology cannot be inferred from them.
- Security/SASE pages move from outcomes into controls, use cases, related stack components, and procurement evidence.
- Audience/solution pages group capabilities by a job before linking to underlying products.
- Product catalogs use filters and repeated cards. Case studies use customer/story selection. Events use region/date browsing. Learning pages use topic hierarchies.
- The architecture destination uses a lighter editorial/catalog treatment, contrasting with newer dark product pages. The brand is not a single page template.
- Status pages prioritize incident severity and chronological updates; they are an operational surface, not visual inspiration for a sales hero.

Some captures were narrow-width; no comprehensive mobile certification follows. Radar was skeletal, community/domain media partly unsettled, and Support redirected to private authentication and was not entered.

**Borrow:** when selling infrastructure, show the relationship between primitives and the larger system, then expose the individual capabilities. A labeled dependency map can be more explanatory than WebGL. Choose spatial rendering only when depth or material helps explain the product.

## Translation into the Cadence SaaS example

Cadence follows one fictional launch rather than introducing a new decorative screenshot in each section:

1. A near-term deadline gives the opening claim a concrete subject.
2. A working workspace exposes people, workstreams, dates, and the actual unresolved task.
3. A dependency map explains handoffs; chapter selection changes the emphasis without scroll hijacking.
4. An editorial pause states the human benefit without inventing performance statistics.
5. The visitor resolves legal review and explicitly approves the launch; shared state updates the product evidence.
6. An illustrative calculator makes the pricing assumption visible.

Exactly two loaded type families—Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans—satisfy the user's current ceiling. Hierarchy comes from measure, scale, spacing, and weight, not additional fonts. DOM/SVG is the selected mechanism here; the LUMA and lab examples retain the separate WebGL/Three.js experiments. More technologies on one page would not demonstrate better reference synthesis.
