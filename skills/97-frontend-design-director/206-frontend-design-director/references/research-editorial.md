# Research, science, and editorial organizations

Use when the site must make a complex thesis understandable and credible.

## Prism pattern

Prism’s site begins with a human-scale scientific problem, explains the limitation of static structures, introduces a methods stack, then connects research to biological outcomes. The visual system uses a pale scientific canvas, violet emphasis, large type, molecular imagery, and restrained data-like graphics.

## Structure

1. A precise mission or unresolved question.
2. Why the current model is insufficient.
3. The organization’s thesis or methods stack.
4. Research areas with diagrams and plain-language summaries.
5. Publications, datasets, tools, or open infrastructure.
6. People and collaborators.
7. Updates / research index.
8. Careers, contact, or participation.

## Rules

- Build a narrative bridge from broad significance to technical mechanism.
- Use diagrams for relationships and processes; use imagery for wonder and scale. Do not ask one visual to do both.
- Define terms near first use without flattening expert content.
- Research entries need type, date, authorship, status, and outbound publication/data links.
- People pages should communicate actual roles and expertise, not only portraits.
- Careers should connect open roles back to the scientific mission and working model.
- Decorative science motifs must derive from the subject: molecular motion, spectra, fields, layers, or measurement—not generic particles.
