# Reference library

Research snapshot: 2026-09-29. The initial version overstated navigation coverage. These are orientation notes, not evidence that every page was studied. See [coverage.md](studies/coverage.md) for individual destination statuses and [visual-studies.md](studies/visual-studies.md) for measured, inspected examples. `content-extracted` means only that page content was retrieved; it does not mean rendered design or interaction review. Some menus and pages remain blocked or incomplete. Do not claim exhaustive coverage or superiority to another skill.

The following interpretations guide reference selection. Measurements and observed behavior take priority when they disagree with these initial notes.

## ElevenLabs — AI platform breadth

Initial navigation themes: Products, Solutions, Customers, Resources, Enterprise, Pricing; key product families ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents, ElevenAPI; customer stories, research, safety, updates.

Transfer: split a broad platform into a few memorable product worlds; make audio/media playable; give creator, agent, and API audiences distinct doors while retaining one research foundation.

## Linear — workflow SaaS

Initial navigation themes: Product, Resources, Customers, Pricing, Now, Contact, Docs; product areas for intake, planning, building, AI/automation, integrations; enterprise and security surfaces.

Transfer: long dark chapters can work when each chapter maps to a real workflow stage and uses high-fidelity product states. Philosophy precedes exhaustive features.

## Stripe — infrastructure and commercial system

Initial navigation themes: Products, Solutions, Developers, Resources, Pricing, product catalog, Enterprise, Startups, Platforms, contact.

Transfer: use a stable grid and editorial illustration to make a huge system legible. Alternate product taxonomy, hard metrics, customer proof, services, and developer detail.

## Perplexity Hub — consumer AI as cultural product

Initial navigation themes: Products, Enterprise, Customers, Pricing, Resources; Answer Engine, Computer, Comet, API Platform.

Transfer: a cinematic, editorial hero can create category-level desire when paired with an immediate functional input and concrete output galleries.

## Supabase — open developer platform

Initial navigation themes: Product, Developers, Solutions, Pricing, Docs, Blog; Database, Auth, Functions, Storage, Realtime, Vector, Data APIs; templates, customer stories, community, open source.

Transfer: two-part promise, calm grid, platform primitives, real code, templates, and community proof. Technical breadth stays approachable through consistent cards and terse language.

## Cloudflare — global infrastructure breadth

Initial navigation themes: Products, Solutions, Resources, Pricing; network, compute/storage, AI, application security, SASE/Zero Trust, plans, case studies, learning.

Transfer: unify enormous breadth under one systems thesis; make scale and network reach tangible; explain pricing with working examples rather than a detached table.

## Firecrawl — focused developer API

Initial navigation themes: product pages for Search, Scrape, Interact, Crawl, Parse, Monitor, Alexandria; use cases, docs, playground, tools, benchmarks, alternatives, changelog, company.

Transfer: technical instrument aesthetic, precise orange syntax, immediate start, code/output demonstrations, and a deep resource architecture for search intent and developer education.

## Cursor — restrained product-first AI

Initial navigation themes: Models, Product, Enterprise, Pricing, Resources; Agents, Cloud, Mobile, Automations, CLI, Review, changelog, blog, docs, community, workshops.

Transfer: restraint can be more distinctive than spectacle. Small claim, large product frame, warm neutral canvas, limited actions, and task-level demonstrations.

## Retool — enterprise product interaction

Initial navigation themes: Solution, Audience, Resources, Use cases, Pricing; Build, Launch, Scale, Govern; AppGen, Agents, workflows, databases, integrations, templates, customer stories, security.

Transfer: make the hero an authentic product interaction. Cinematic background creates urgency; visible input and generated application explain the product; enterprise governance immediately resolves the risk.

## Ramp — outcome-led fintech

Initial navigation themes: Products, Partners, Solutions, Resources, Customers, Pricing; cards, expenses, AP, procurement, banking, intelligence, integrations, global, tools.

Transfer: plainspoken outcome, bright conversion color, quantified operational proof, exhaustive use-case architecture, role-specific customer evidence, and clear disclosures.

## Mercury — aspirational fintech

Initial navigation themes: Products, Solutions, Resources, About, Pricing; banking, credit, spend, payments, invoicing, accounting, intelligence, vertical solutions, tools, company and support.

Transfer: cinematic nature and object imagery can humanize finance without sacrificing clarity. Follow atmosphere quickly with concrete jobs, controls, yields/fees, eligibility, and security.

## Prism Science — research narrative

Initial navigation themes: About; Science overview, Data Collection, Modeling, Infrastructure, Biological Impact; Research, People, Open Roles.

Transfer: make the scientific gap the protagonist, connect methods to consequences, use subject-derived motion/refraction, and treat research, people, and careers as first-class content systems.

## Content Architecture — technical product with a governing metaphor

Initial navigation themes: Features, The Repo, Showcase, Pricing, FAQ, Blog, Roadmap, access.

Transfer: repository and terminal language governs copy, graphics, nav, and section mechanics. Visible implementation detail becomes the proof.

## Blink — high-concept launch

Initial navigation themes: one-page launch and Docs; sections for proposition, why Blink, physical infrastructure, equal access, verifiability, regulated markets, and mechanism.

Transfer: a short launch can feel complete when one physical idea—network distance—drives spatial composition, data labels, animation, and copy.

## Edoardo Lunardi / Prism case study — portfolio workbench

Initial navigation themes: Home, Works, Blog, Lab, Awards; Prism case study and work index.

Transfer: a dense desktop/workbench metaphor supports fast comparison across many projects. Case study metadata and artifacts remain immediately visible despite the experimental shell.
