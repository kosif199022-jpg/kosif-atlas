# Archetype routing

Classify by buying psychology, not industry label alone.

## Fast diagnostic

Ask in order:

1. What is being chosen: a workflow, infrastructure, financial relationship, physical object, body of knowledge, or point of view?
2. Is the visitor’s main friction comprehension, trust, desire, technical feasibility, or memorability?
3. What can prove value fastest: interface, output, code, metric, photograph, diagram, publication, or case study?
4. Is the conversion self-serve, sales-led, purchase, application, subscription, contact, or reputation?

## Mixed cases

- AI SaaS for developers: developer-platform structure with product-led demonstrations.
- Enterprise SaaS: SaaS product proof with enterprise trust and implementation detail.
- Fintech for consumers: fintech credibility with consumer clarity and emotional benefit.
- Ecommerce technology product: commerce desire first; technical substantiation second.
- Research organization recruiting talent: research narrative first; people/careers conversion second.
- Creative developer portfolio: portfolio selection with experimental interaction, not a SaaS funnel.

## Confidence rule

If no archetype reaches roughly 70% confidence, state the assumed primary mode before building. Do not blend every reference family to avoid choosing.
