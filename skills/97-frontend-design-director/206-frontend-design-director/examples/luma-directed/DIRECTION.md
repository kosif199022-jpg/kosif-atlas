# LUMA, directed — a test of our new skill

Governing workflow: **Frontend Design Director**, repository v0.2.0, plus the later source-code audit of Prism and Pear. The previous LUMA at `../cinematic/` used the classic frontend-design and awwwards-motion guidance. It is preserved unchanged for comparison. This rebuild is not a controlled blind evaluation and does not prove superiority.

## Routing and concept

Primary archetype: physical product. Secondary influence: editorial launch. This site should feel like an optical field journal because photographers need to understand the instrument, enjoy its material detail, and imagine the attention it invites.

This is a fictional design study. No price, availability, optical benchmark, customer testimonial, real mount compatibility, or photographic performance is invented. The prototype conversion is selecting a finish and inspecting the original 3D model. Any field image is clearly identified as generated concept imagery, not a photograph produced by the fictional instrument.

## Two compositions

**A — Optical field journal, selected.** Bone-white opening. A small, factual product introduction above a monumental serif product designation, offset matte-metal lens render and annotated scale. A deliberate dark cut opens a technical construction chapter. Full-bleed editorial imagery then changes the proof medium. A compact specimen selector closes with a real material choice. Different compositions have different jobs.

**B — Continuous dark object film, rejected.** One model floating across four black stages, with headline/copy pairs changing sides. This repeats the previous version's hierarchy and offers little physical-product context. Smooth scene changes alone do not solve that problem.

## Reference evidence

Prism's source-confirmed shared scroll state coordinates object, camera, and explanatory text. Blink's observed annotations place text around the explained object. Mercury's art-directed environment transitions to concrete operations. Stripe supplies alignment discipline across expressive and informational sections. Cursor demonstrates that product proof need not compete with oversized marketing copy. Pear informs subject continuity and editorial crop, not wholesale reproduction of its media pipeline. See the library's measured studies and the local reference-code audit; coverage remains incomplete and no entire-site implementation is claimed.

## Art direction

Typography: Bodoni Moda roman for the monumental product title and editorial proposition; Archivo for navigation, explanatory headings, and body; DM Mono for specimen codes and measurements. Three families have distinct roles; no alternating italic word, condensed all-caps slogan, or Manrope/Instrument house style.

At 1280×720: 48px margins, a 76px header, 12-column implied grid. Hero 100svh minimum. Product designation 190–240px occupies the lower-left; the lens occupies the center-right, approximately 480px across. Header, product introduction, specimen labels and CTA have independent clear zones. Hero title uses 0.9 leading. Construction changes to charcoal and cream, small index at left, mechanism on the broad left region, 34–44px heading plus controls at right. The field image is full-bleed and followed by a quiet caption, not a SaaS feature grid.

Mobile: explicit 40/60 copy/object distribution in the first screen; title scales to 24vw. The mechanism moves above the controls rather than trapping text under the canvas. Finish choice is native radio input. No scroll hijacking, hover-only meaning, or canvas-only product information.

## Behaviors

One renderer relocates between observed stage rectangles. Shared scroll progress opens the assembly in the construction chapter; selecting a component emphasizes it and changes the explanation. The finish selector changes model materials and semantic output. Reduced motion freezes scroll choreography but keeps component selection and material changes. Render only when dirty or settling; pause when hidden/offscreen; provide an authored SVG fallback for no-WebGL or context loss.

## Required review

Capture desktop opening, construction/changed state, and narrow mobile. Exercise component buttons, keyboard finish selection, chapter navigation, reduced-motion toggle, fallback, and static content. Record checks and failures in REVIEW.md before presenting.
