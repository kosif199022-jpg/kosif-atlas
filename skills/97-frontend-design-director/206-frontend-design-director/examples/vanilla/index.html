02 / The method

Conviction is built.
Not conjured.

You don’t need another score pretending to know the answer. You need a clear view of what you know, what you don’t, and what happens next.

01

Keep the source.

Carry the customer’s words, the cohort, and the date. A signal is only useful if you can trace it.

↗
02

Hold both sides.

Place supporting and contrary evidence together. See where a promising idea needs a narrower scope.

⇄
03

Leave a way back.

Name an owner and a review trigger. Future you should know what would change your mind.
